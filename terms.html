Livegift Terms of Use

  • Offensive, sexual, violent, hateful, harassing content or anything harming children is forbidden and not tolerated.
  • Post only content you own or have permission to use.
  • Users can report content and block users; reports are reviewed and violating content or accounts are removed or banned.
  • Purchased coins cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. Some features, such as purchases and creator payouts, may not be available yet. Where available, creator payouts follow the payout rules (minimum amount, minimum account age, manual review). Fraud (fake accounts, gifting yourself, fake engagement) voids earnings and leads to a ban. Creators are responsible for their own taxes. Purchases are made through Apple and refunds follow Apple's rules. Livegift may change prices, creator share and bonuses.
  • If a VIP subscription is offered, it renews automatically unless cancelled in your Apple ID settings at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • The minimum age is 13; going live and sending gifts are for users 18 and older.
  • Lives are checked automatically and by people: strike 1 is a warning; from strike 2 the live is closed and live access is paused for 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, then 7 days; serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent ban. Smoking, drugs, weapons, nudity, violence and a driver going live while driving are not allowed (passengers are fine).
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings.
